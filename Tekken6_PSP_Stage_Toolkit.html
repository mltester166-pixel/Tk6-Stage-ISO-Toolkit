Quick guide

  1. ISO: open your PSP ISO or drop it on the page. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Extractor / Models: search a stage by name, then open it.
  3. Textures: models point at textures by ID. Scan the ISO to find them, search by ID, or assign one by hand.
  4. Models: orbit the stage's objects, textured, solid, wireframe or points.
  5. Extractor: export models as OBJ, unpack the stage to a ZIP, or import an OBJ or .ndpp over a model.
  6. Save: export an ISO copy, or write straight into the ISO.

Tips

  • OBJ import keeps the model's header and materials and rewrites its geometry (triangles, UVs). Keep face counts modest.
  • Replacing a model can grow the stage. If it no longer fits its ISO slot you will be told before anything changes.
  • Changes stay in memory until you save. Keep a backup of your ISO.
